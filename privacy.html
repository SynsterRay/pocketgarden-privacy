Privacy Policy

Pocket Garden: Merge · Wonder Minds Games · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Pocket Garden: Merge ("the game", "we", "us") handles information when you play. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game, process purchases, show ads and understand how the game is played so we can improve it. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

Information the game handles

Data stored on your device

Your progress — merge board, player level and experience, coins, gems, energy, garden decorations, quests, achievements, daily-bonus streak and your settings (language, music and sound) — is stored locally on your device (Android SharedPreferences / PlayerPrefs). Your save itself is not uploaded to any server, and it is removed when you uninstall the game. Separately, anonymous usage statistics are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase - see Analytics below.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. When you buy something, Google handles the transaction and payment details; we never see or store your payment information. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded, so we can grant the item in the game. Purchases are optional.

The optional VIP Pass is a monthly auto-renewing subscription billed through Google Play. You can cancel it at any time in the Google Play app (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions); it stays active until the end of the paid period.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (a Google service) to understand how players use the game - for example where new players stop, how long a session lasts and which features are used - so we can fix problems and improve the game. Firebase may collect:

  • app-instance identifiers generated by Firebase and, where permitted, your advertising identifier;
  • general device information (device model, operating-system version, language, coarse region derived from your IP address - your IP address itself is not stored);
  • app usage: first launch, sessions and their length, and in-game events such as completing the tutorial, reaching a level, finishing a request, building a decoration, opening the shop, making or cancelling a purchase, watching an ad or sharing your garden, together with game values such as your player level and currency balances;
  • purchase events (product and price) reported by Google Play.

This data is not linked to your name, email or other contact details, and we do not use it to identify you. It is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game submits your scores (Garden Score, total stars, player level) to its leaderboards and reports unlocked achievements. Your Play Games profile name and avatar may be visible to other players on those leaderboards, according to your Play Games settings. This is handled by Google; signing in is optional.

Sharing your garden

When you tap Share, the game creates a picture of your garden on your device and opens Android's share sheet. The picture and text go only to the app you choose; we do not receive or store them. The image is kept in the game's temporary cache and replaced the next time you share.

Advertising

The game shows advertising through Google AdMob (a Google service). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / App Set ID);
  • general device information (device model, operating-system version, coarse region/language);
  • ad-interaction data (impressions, clicks) used to measure and, where permitted, personalise ads.

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. You can review and control it here: Google Ads & Privacy. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

Notifications

The game can send you local reminders (for example, when your energy has refilled or your daily bonus is ready). These are scheduled on your device and are not sent from any server. On Android 13 and newer we ask for notification permission the first time you play; you can decline, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play Games) strictly to run those features. Those providers process the data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Opt out of personalised ads and limit analytics use of your advertising ID by turning on Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization in your device settings.
  • Play without signing in to Google Play Games.
  • Turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the game to remove all locally stored data.
  • Make purchases optional — the game is fully playable without them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version. Continued play after an update means you accept the revised policy.
